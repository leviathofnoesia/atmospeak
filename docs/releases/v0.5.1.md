# Atmospeak v0.5.1

Atmospeak 0.5.1 delivers the latency and polish that 0.5.0 promised: streaming
still transcribes while you speak, but stop-to-paste is fast again, paste
targets keep their geometry, and the dock stays reachable.

## Highlights

- Splits the ASR sidecar into a reader thread and an inference worker so
  microphone frames never block behind VAD or preview decodes.
- Prioritizes `StopSession` over previews and runs Silero only on the trailing
  three seconds of the uncommitted chunk.
- Overlaps host finalization with capture teardown, tolerates micro-drops, and
  keeps a lazy resident `whisper-server` ready for batch fallback.
- Leaves maximized and normal paste targets alone — only minimized windows are
  restored before Ctrl+V.
- Clamps the dock on drag end and persists the settled position so it cannot
  strand fully off-screen.

## Reliability

This release also closes the writer-queue drop-accounting hole that could paste
a truncated streaming final without triggering fallback, and reports material
drops in `StreamingMetrics` instead of discarding the whole streamed transcript
on a single 20 ms gap.
