When the Tauri app exits, this thread keeps running for up to 300 seconds (the sleep interval) before the OS kills it. This causes a delay on app shutdown and can leave SQLite handles open past the app's lifetime. Adding a cancellation token (using `AtomicBool` shared with the `AppState`) allows the sweeper to exit immediately when the app shuts down.## Current state- `src-tauri/src/services/runtime.rs:47-58` —
