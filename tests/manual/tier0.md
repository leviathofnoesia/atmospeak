# Tier 0 Manual AcceptanceUse this script after `bun run build`, `bun run test`, and `cargo test` pass.## Setup1. Start Atmospeak with `bun run tauri dev`.2. Complete onboarding if prompted.3. Open Notepad and focus the document body.## T0-01 Copy-To-Clipboard Output Mode1. Open Settings.2. Enable Auto-inject after transcription.3. Set Injection mode to Copy to clipboard only and save.4. Dictate into Notepad.5. Confirm Atmospeak does not send the paste shortcut.6. Press `Ctrl+V` manually and confirm the transcript is on the clipboard.7. Restore Injection mode to Auto-paste into focused app and save.## T0-02 Custom Instructions1. Open Advanced.2. Enter text in Custom instructions and save.3. Switch away from Advanced and back.4. Confirm the saved instructions remain.## T0-03 Model Selector1. Open Advanced.2. Confirm the model list includes Tiny English, Base English, Small English, Medium English, Large v3, and Distil Large v3.3. Confirm Base English is installed and selectable.4. Confirm unavailable models are visible but disabled until installed.5. Save settings and confirm the status remains ready with `base.en`.## T0-04 History Search And Filters1. Dictate at least two short transcripts in different target apps, or use existing history with multiple sessions.2. Open History.3. Search for a word contained in only one transcript and confirm only matching rows remain.4. Set From and To dates around a known session date and confirm rows outside the range are hidden.5. Set Min words and Max words and confirm rows outside the word-count range are hidden.6. Clear all filters and confirm the full recent history list returns.## T0-05 Export Transcript1. Expand a History row.2. Click TXT, MD, JSON, and SRT export controls.3. Confirm each download contains the transcript text.4. Confirm the SRT export has a `00:00:00,000 --> HH:MM:SS,mmm` cue.## T0-06 Floating Bubble Appearance1. Open Settings.2. Change Floating bubble size to Small and save.3. Show the floating control and confirm it uses the smaller layout.4. Change Floating bubble size to Large and lower opacity, then save.5. Confirm the floating control grows and becomes more transparent.## T0-07 History Stats1. Expand a History row.2. Confirm word count, duration, WPM, and app are visible.## T0-08 Recent Apps1. Dictate into at least two different foreground apps.2. Open History.3. Confirm Top apps shows the captured app names and categories.## T0-09 Audio Replay1. Expand a History row created from a real recording.2. Use the audio player and confirm playback starts from the saved recording.## T0-10 Tray Quick Stats1. Complete a dictation session.2. Hover the tray icon.3. Confirm the tooltip includes words today, session count, and last-session duration.## Regression Checks1. Expand a History row and confirm words, duration, WPM, app, notes, audio replay, and TXT/MD/JSON/SRT export controls are still visible.2. Use Copy and Paste again actions from a History row and confirm they do not expand or collapse the row unintentionally.3. In Settings, switch Injection mode between Auto paste and Clipboard only, save, and confirm dictation still completes.
